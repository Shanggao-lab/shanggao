Hi! I'm Shang Gao.

I am an assistant professor at the Wang Yanan Institute for Studies in Economics (WISE), Xiamen University.

My research focuses on theoretical econometrics, high-dimensional factor models, and machine learning, with applications in macroeconomics and finance.

Before joining WISE, I was a postdoctoral researcher at the School of Economics and Management, Tsinghua University. I received my Ph.D. in Economics from Fudan University in 2024, and my M.A. and B.A. in Economics from Lingnan College, Sun Yat-sen University in 2020 and 2018, respectively.
